Pure Puzzle: ad-free jigsaw

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android app Pure Puzzle: ad-free jigsaw (short name Pure Puzzle; the Google Play title varies slightly by language), package name com.robert.purepuzzle, published on Google Play by the developer Robert Masur. In this policy, "the app" means this app, and "the developer", "I" and "me" mean Robert Masur.

The app does not collect, store, share, or sell personal data.

Data Collection

The app does not:

  • require account creation
  • collect personal information
  • collect contact details
  • collect location data
  • collect financial information
  • collect health information
  • collect messages, photos, or files from the device
  • use analytics or tracking tools
  • use advertising SDKs

Your Own Photos

You can choose to add your own pictures as puzzle motifs. A picture you select is cropped and copied into the app's private storage on your device, and nowhere else. It is never uploaded or sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the motif in the app, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

Purchases

The app is offered as a paid app download through Google Play. Payment processing is handled by Google Play, not by the app itself. The app does not process or store payment information.

Ratings and Reviews

The app may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review feature. This is provided by Google Play on your device; the app does not receive or store your rating, your review, or any information about you. Google's handling of that data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is a simple offline puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults.

Internet and Third Parties

The app does not include:

  • in-app advertising
  • social features
  • third-party analytics
  • third-party marketing SDKs

The only links that leave the app are in the "For parents" area, which is protected by a press-and-hold gate. They open this privacy policy in your browser and the app's page in the Google Play Store.

Device Storage

The app stores only the app resources and local gameplay data required for normal operation on the device, such as puzzle progress, settings, and your own motifs. This data stays on the device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Data Sharing

The app does not share any personal data with third parties because the app does not collect personal data.

Security

Because the app does not collect account or personal data, privacy risk is intentionally minimized by design.

Changes to This Policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated before or at the time the changes are released.
