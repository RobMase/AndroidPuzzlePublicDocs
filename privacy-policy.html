Pure Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

Pure Puzzle does not collect, store, share, or sell personal data.

Data Collection

Pure Puzzle does not:

  • require account creation
  • collect personal information
  • collect contact details
  • collect location data
  • collect financial information
  • collect health information
  • collect messages, photos, or files from the device
  • use analytics or tracking tools
  • use advertising SDKs

Purchases

Pure Puzzle is offered as a paid app download through Google Play. Payment processing is handled by Google Play, not by the app itself. Pure Puzzle does not process or store payment information.

Children

Pure Puzzle is a simple offline puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults.

Internet and Third Parties

Pure Puzzle does not include:

  • in-app advertising
  • external links
  • social features
  • third-party analytics
  • third-party marketing SDKs

Device Storage

Pure Puzzle stores only the app resources and local gameplay data required for normal operation on the device. This data stays on the device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Data Sharing

Pure Puzzle does not share any personal data with third parties because the app does not collect personal data.

Security

Because Pure Puzzle does not collect account or personal data, privacy risk is intentionally minimized by design.

Changes to This Policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated before or at the time the changes are released.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the contact details provided on the Google Play listing.

This page is intended to be published publicly, for example via GitHub Pages, and used as the privacy policy URL in Google Play Console.
